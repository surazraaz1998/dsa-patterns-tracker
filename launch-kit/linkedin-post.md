I built a free interview-prep app that shows you how algorithms run, step by step, instead of just giving you the code. 🚀

Most prep material hands you a solution. Interviews ask you to explain *why* it works. So I put together a 6-month roadmap across four tracks — DSA, High-Level Design, Low-Level Design and Agentic AI — where every topic is taught in the same simple format:

✅ The idea in plain words, with a real-life analogy
✅ A deep dive: how it works, why it's correct, edge cases
✅ A step-by-step visualiser you can play, pause and feed your own input — every sorting method, binary search, two pointers, sliding window, linked lists, trees, BFS/DFS and DP tables
✅ YouTube clips that play inside the app — only the part about that exact concept, not a 2-hour lecture
✅ A Python/JavaScript template, common mistakes, interview tips and a short quiz

What's inside today:
• 41 full lessons (Weeks 1–16 of the roadmap), with the rest outlined
• 29 interactive visualisations
• 277 hand-picked video clips from creators like Striver, NeetCode, Abdul Bari, ByteByteGo and Hello Interview
• An interview-day playbook for DSA, HLD and LLD rounds

It's free and works without an account — progress is saved in your browser and syncs if you sign in.

Built with React, TypeScript, FastAPI and Postgres. All the content is plain JSON, so adding or improving a lesson is a one-file PR.
