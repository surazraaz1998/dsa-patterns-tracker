## Recording tips

- Record on a real phone (iOS/Android screen recording) at **Slow** speed in the visualiser, then speed up 1.5–2× in editing — smoother than the screenshot-based draft.
- Use the "Use mine" box to type a memorable array (e.g. `5, 1, 4, 2, 8`) so viewers can follow.
- Keep captions in the middle third so Instagram's UI doesn't cover them.
- Cover image: the BFS graph mid-run or the sorting bars with yellow highlights + "Watch it run".
